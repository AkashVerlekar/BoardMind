# User Guide

BoardMind empowers executives to make data-driven decisions swiftly.

## Dashboard Walkthrough
The **Morning Brief** is the primary landing page. Upon loading, the platform dynamically:
1. Calculates trailing KPIs.
2. Formulates the Business Health Score.
3. Detects anomalies.
4. Generates AI recommendations.

## Business Health
Located in the upper section of the Dashboard, this module provides an aggregate score out of 100 based on three weighted dimensions:
- **Financial** (Revenue & Profit)
- **Customers** (Acquisition & Churn)
- **Employees** (Burnout Risk)
Scores below 70 trigger "Warning" statuses, while scores below 50 trigger "Critical" statuses.

## Analytics
The Analytics section visualizes trailing 30-day historical data. Data is sourced directly from the Enterprise Simulator, reflecting a chronological narrative of the simulated company's financial performance.

## Recommendations Workflow
AI-generated insights appear in the Recommendations module. Executives can:
- **Approve / Reject:** Vet the AI's proposal.
- **Assign:** Select a registered employee from the simulated directory to take ownership of the task.
- **Complete:** Mark the objective as done, closing the loop.
